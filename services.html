Fleet Services

Practical support for your whole fleet

From tracking and installation to alerts and reporting, explore services that help you stay informed about vehicles, drivers and equipment.

Vehicle and fleet tracking

View the latest reported positions of vehicles on a shared fleet map. Review trip history, stops and travel distances over a selected period to help with dispatching and day-to-day planning.

Useful for: checking vehicle activity, reviewing routes and coordinating field teams.

Geofencing and zone management

Set up virtual zones for depots, customer sites or restricted areas. Where configured, entry and exit events help your team check arrivals, monitor site visits and spot unexpected movement.

Useful for: location-based notifications and checking planned visits.

Driver identification

Associate journeys with a driver using compatible RFID, iButton or driver-terminal identification. Driver records give managers more context when reviewing trips and vehicle events.

May require: compatible identification hardware and configured driver accounts.

Fuel and vehicle telemetry

For supported vehicles, review fuel levels and available operating data such as RPM, odometer readings or engine temperature. Reports can help identify changes that may need further investigation.

May require: compatible vehicle data, fuel sensors and correct installation or calibration.

Alerts and incident notifications

Configure available alerts for events such as geofence changes, speeding, unexpected movement, power loss or possible tampering. Notifications help teams review events promptly; they do not replace emergency services or direct vehicle supervision.

Delivery depends on: tracker settings, network coverage and enabled notification channels.

Vehicle security support

Supported panic buttons, alerts and security accessories can help raise awareness of an incident and provide vehicle information for follow-up. Remote immobilisation is available only on compatible installations and should be used by authorised operators when safe and lawful.

Good to know: tracking supports response but cannot guarantee theft prevention or recovery.

Driver behaviour monitoring

Where supported by the tracker, review events such as harsh braking, acceleration, cornering, speeding and excessive idling. Use trip context and coaching conversations to identify patterns and support safer driving practices.

Useful for: fleet reviews, driver coaching and identifying repeat events.

Web and mobile access

Authorised users can access the tracking platform through a supported browser or mobile app to check vehicle activity while away from the office. Account permissions help control which information and tools each user can access.

Access depends on: supported devices, connectivity and account permissions.

Fleet reports and dashboards

Bring available trip, distance, stop, driver and telemetry information together for a selected vehicle or group. Use reports to understand usage patterns and inform operational decisions.

Report details depend on: installed equipment, data availability and your platform configuration.

System integration

Discuss connecting tracking data with your existing ERP, CRM or operational tools. Integration options, available fields and any mileage- or usage-based workflows are assessed against the systems involved.

Next step: share your systems and the data you want to exchange for a feasibility review.

Connected tracking devices

Tracking devices use GPS to determine location and a supported mobile network to send updates to the platform. Reporting frequency and coverage vary with the device, network availability and operating environment.

Good to know: live information depends on the device being powered and able to report.
