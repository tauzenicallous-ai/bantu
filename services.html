Services

Check our Services

We provide a five star service delivery 24/7 to your door step.

Geo Zone Management

We offer Geo fencing capabilities which enable our clients to configure preferred areas, no go areas and waypoints for their fleet to adhere to.

Driver Identification

Bantu track system allows you to identify individual drivers through RFID or driver terminal PIN. The system also allows for seatbelt and door sensors to enforce vehicle safety and security

Vehicle and Fleet Tracking

The system offers real-time tracking of the fleet which records vehicle position and locations at predefined intervals, with trip playback facilities. It should be able to calculate the number of kilometers travelled over a certain period.

Seamless Integration with Third Party Systems

possible to integrate the system to third party software (ERP, CRM, etc.) for data sharing and automated billing based on mileage, hours, and days

Stolen Vehicle Response

The system assists in preventing vehicle thefts through panic buttons, sirens activation to signal hijackings and thefts

Incident Alerts and Alarms

The system has alerts for high force accidents, rollovers, system tampering, loss of power, battery tampering, vehicle movements while switched off, and zone violations.

WEB Based Monitoring Via Smartphone & Tablet

The system should be accessible from handheld devices such as smartphones and tablets twenty-four hours a day. One should be able to operate and configure the devices from their phone.

Rich Fleet Analytics and Dashboards

The system records and stores all and any activity concerning the car, so that the client can get a full picture of the car’s activities on one page

Multiple Communication Channels

The system communicates through GPS and/or GPRS in addition to other channels that may be available. The system device is be able to send data from any location in Zimbabwe and abroad.

Fuel Consumption Monitoring

Clients are able to remotely access performance and usage data of the vehicle on a wide range of information including fuel consumption (km/L), revolutions per minute (RPM), odometer reading (ODO), throttle position engine load/torque, fuel levels and engine temperature.

Driver Behaviour Monitoring

System allows client to be able to monitor driver behaviour through the system by monitoring such events as acceleration, harsh braking, cornering, speeding as well as harsh bumps, high g-force incidents and excessive idling. This should help the Ministry to develop and give feedback to its drivers on areas of improvements
