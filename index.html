About

Find Out More About Us

Inspired by the Bantu values of innovation, accountability and integrity, Bantu Track Pvt Ltd was founded in 2014 and has grown into one of Zimbabwe's largest vehicle tracking and telemetry providers. We help clients improve efficiency and business growth through effective asset management and reliable telemetry. Our system is accessible 24/7 on computers, laptops, phones and tablets. Our Android and iOS apps let you track assets in real time and receive alerts of your choice.

What we can do

Our vehicle tracking system lets users monitor the location of vehicles and trailers. GPS data gives businesses real-time visibility of fleet movements.

What the system can do

Bantu Track's vehicle tracking system lets you monitor and manage vehicles from a computer, smartphone or tablet, 24/7. Features include geofencing, fuel monitoring, alerts and SMS, location detection, and engine blocking.
