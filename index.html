About Bantu Track

Local expertise. Smarter fleet visibility.

Founded in Zimbabwe in 2014, Bantu Track combines GPS tracking and vehicle telemetry to help businesses understand how their vehicles and equipment are being used.

Our story

Technology that works for real-world operations

Bantu Track helps organisations bring vehicle location, trip activity and available telemetry into one accessible tracking platform. Teams can review fleet activity, set relevant alerts and use the information to make more informed day-to-day decisions.

Our solutions support different types of assets, from cars and commercial vehicles to compatible machinery. The information available depends on the installed device, vehicle, sensors and network coverage.
